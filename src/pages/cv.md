## Professional Summary

Deliver end-to-end service design and mixed methods research in data-rich, regulated financial services. Make complexity—across people, process, policy, data, and technology—legible and actionable, enabling senior leaders in Product, Operations, Engineering, and Data to reduce failure demand, mitigate risk, and improve throughput. Specialise in translating ambiguity into measurable outcomes that drive efficiency, adoption, and quality from discovery through delivery, with strong focus on testing, delivery readiness, and design system adoption.



## Core Skills

- Lead end-to-end service design for regulated financial services, producing actionable outputs
- Map journeys and service blueprints to identify failure demand, process friction, and efficiency opportunities
- Conduct mixed methods research (qualitative, quantitative, evaluative testing) for robust, evidence-led decision making
- Facilitate senior stakeholder alignment, resolving competing priorities and unlocking delivery
- Optimise risk, controls, and operational frameworks to align with business targets and regulatory requirements
- Translate research into flows, wireframes, prototypes, and specifications suitable for rapid implementation
- Embed accessibility and design system standards to ensure scalable, compliant experiences
- Deliver cross-functional results with Product, Ops, Engineering, Data, and Compliance, measured by throughput/adoption gains
- Drive commercial insight to support sales and product growth targets
- Build capability and throughput by mentoring, leading workshops, and developing reusable toolkits
